Questions sellers ask

What is an effective fee rate?

The effective fee rate is every fee a platform charges on one order divided by the order price. It is the number to compare across platforms, because a 6.5% transaction fee plus 3% + 25¢ processing plus a 20¢ listing fee is about 10% of a $30 order, not 6.5%.

Why does the same platform charge different amounts at different prices?

Fixed fees — card fees per order, listing fees, fulfilment fees, monthly plans — do not scale with price. On a $10 sale a 30¢ card fee is 3% of the order; on a $100 sale it is 0.3%. That is why this site computes fees at 12 price points instead of quoting one headline percentage.

Do these numbers include shipping and sales tax?

They model the item price only, with the buyer paying shipping and tax separately. Etsy, eBay, Mercari, Depop and TikTok Shop charge their percentage on shipping too, and eBay, Etsy and Depop charge it on the sales tax the buyer pays — both are described on each platform page and both raise the real rate.

How current are the rates?

Every rate was read from the platform's own pricing or fee page on 11 November 2026. Platform fees change without notice; the page each figure comes from is linked on the rates and sources page so you can check it before you price.
