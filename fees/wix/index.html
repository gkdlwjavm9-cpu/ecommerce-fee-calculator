How Wix fees actually work

Wix's Business plans do not take a commission on sales in the US; you pay the monthly plan and card processing of about 2.9% + 30¢ through Wix Payments or a connected gateway.

The plan tier decides what you can sell (subscriptions, abandoned-cart recovery, multi-currency) rather than the fee. As with any hosted storefront, a fixed monthly cost per order falls fast as volume grows and is punishing at 10 orders a month.

Best for

Small catalogues and service businesses already building a site in Wix

Watch out for

Plan prices rise with features, and Wix Payments is 2.9% + 30¢; some regions and plans still carry a per-transaction fee.

Questions

What are Wix fees on a $50 sale?

Fees come to $1.93 on a $50 sale, or 3.9% of the price, leaving you $48.07 before the cost of the product. The line-by-line breakdown is on the $50 page.

What is the break-even ROAS on Wix?

1.04x on the sale price alone, and 1.51x once a product cost of 30% of the price is included. Break-even ROAS is the sale price divided by the money the order leaves you — below that multiple, paid traffic loses money.

Does Wix charge on shipping?

The fee here is modelled on the item price. Check the platform's page for whether shipping you charge is included — on most marketplaces it is.

Is Wix cheaper than a marketplace for small sellers?

At $50 the total is $1.93. Whether that is cheaper depends on the monthly cost per order and on whether the channel brings its own buyers: a storefront keeps more per sale but you pay for the traffic.
