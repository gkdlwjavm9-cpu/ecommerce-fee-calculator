Questions

Is BigCommerce cheaper than Shopify?

Shopify is cheaper at all 6 price points — BigCommerce is never cheaper on fees alone, though it brings its own buyers. At $50 the totals are $1.95 on BigCommerce and $1.95 on Shopify.

Which is better for a $50 product?

On fees alone, Shopify — $1.95 against $1.95. Neither figure includes the cost of getting the buyer there, which is where the two models actually differ.

Do these numbers include ads or boosts?

No. Both are modelled with no advertising: no Etsy Offsite Ads, no Promoted Listings, no TikTok affiliate commission and no paid traffic. Each of those can add 10–20% to the order.
