How these numbers are calculated

Every fee on this site is read from the platform's own pricing or fee page, then applied with the same model so the comparison is fair. This page lists the assumptions, the sources and what the model deliberately leaves out.

Advertisement

The model

  1. One item, one order. Item price only; the buyer pays shipping and sales tax separately unless the platform charges its fee on them, in which case that is stated on the page.
  2. US seller, US buyer, standard category. Category exceptions (Amazon electronics at 8%, Walmart jewellery at 20%, apparel price tiers) are named on the platform page rather than averaged away.
  3. No advertising. Etsy Offsite Ads, eBay Promoted Listings, TikTok affiliate commission and paid traffic are described, never charged.
  4. Monthly plans spread over 200 orders. A $39 plan adds 19.5¢ to an order at 200 orders a month and $3.90 at 10. The calculator lets you change the order count, because this assumption changes the ranking.
  5. Amazon FBA fulfilment at $4.15. A standard-size unit around 1 lb. Fulfilment fees run roughly $3.06–$5.50 by size tier, so a heavier product costs more than this page shows.
  6. Product cost at 30% of price for the contribution and COGS-adjusted ROAS lines. Use your own number in the calculator.

What is left out

Returns and refunds (some platforms keep the fee, some refund it), storage and long-term storage, removal and disposal fees, currency conversion, chargebacks, subscription and app costs, tax and bookkeeping. These matter — a 20% return rate can cost more than the difference between two platforms — but they are seller-specific, so they belong in your own model, not in a comparison table.
